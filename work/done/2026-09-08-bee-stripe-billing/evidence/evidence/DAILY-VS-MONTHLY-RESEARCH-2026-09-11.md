# Bee free quota: daily versus monthly

Status: research and recommendation, not policy activation. Recorded 2026-09-11 21:31 EDT. Owner proposes 100 free reads daily, resetting daily, with wait-or-pay at exhaustion and asks whether research supports it. Prior owner-account parity release correction still governs: quota work does not block shipment.

## Finding

Recommend 100 successful Bee reads per account per calendar day as a provisional candidate for the stated recurring-free-use goal. This is product judgment, not proven optimal conversion. Targeted searches found no direct causal comparison establishing daily over monthly freemium reset superiority; the prior research does not supply one. Earlier preference for a monthly pool was based on accommodating bursty use, not measured superiority.

100/day yields up to 3,000 reads in a 30-day month, with no rollover assumed for comparison. That changes both the total and cadence versus the earlier 1,000/month proposal. Equal-volume comparison is 100/day versus 3,000/month. With 50/day, daily permits all 1,500 monthly reads; 1,000/month exhausts on day20. A person doing 300 reads on one day monthly fits 1,000/month but hits 100/day. These are arithmetic examples, not observed users. Daily shortens the wait to the next daily boundary, while restricting bursts. It could improve return use or reduce urgency to subscribe; conversion direction is unproven.
