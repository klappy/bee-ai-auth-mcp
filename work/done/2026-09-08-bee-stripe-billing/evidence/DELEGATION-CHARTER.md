- A reviewer authors the same change it then claims to independently validate.

- Correct the checkpoint to the observed state and obtain the missing receipt before the dependent action.
- Stop only the affected unauthorized transition; retain existing work and name its exact gate.

- Obtain a fresh-context review; do not relabel self-review as independent validation.
