Access & privacy

What's held, what isn't, and how to revoke.

Custody is the whole question with a credential relay. On Chris's hosted service, your Bee credential is held in an encrypted grant on infrastructure Chris operates. You trust the operator and hosting infrastructure to handle that access; retrieved material also reaches your AI provider.

Two legs, kept separate

The login leg (you ↔ relay) and the data leg (relay ↔ Bee) never mix. GitHub authenticates who you are and grants nothing else — no repository access, the service reads your login for the access check and discards the GitHub token. Your Bee token rides the other leg only — captured at consent and held in your own encrypted grant, never a shared secret.

What gets stored

Bee credentials are held in the OAuth provider's grants, in the deployment's OAUTH_KV namespace. Grant props are encrypted per-grant with key material derived from the token, not a shared KMS key. Encryption at rest is not a guarantee against compromise of the running service.

The Bee bearer

It lives only in your own encrypted grant props — never a shared Worker secret. The service is designed to keep credentials out of logs, URLs, errors and tool output. The exposed Bee tools perform read-only retrieval. Retrieved conversation data passes through the service to your AI provider.

Revocation — said plainly

Disconnect Bee in your AI app to stop using it there. This does not erase material already included in chats or guarantee revocation of the credential at Bee. For help removing hosted access or revoking Bee authorization, ask Chris.

Who can use the hosted service

Every login is checked against ALLOWED_GITHUB_LOGIN. Chris manages the list of invited GitHub usernames. Each allowed user connects their own Bee account.

Hosted access and self-hosting

The hosted service is for invited friends and family. Encryption at rest does not remove trust in the operator: the service handles your credential and retrieved data while making requests. Self-hosting puts deployment control in your hands, along with responsibility for running and securing it.

Limits of this invitation-only service

This is not a claim that the broader hosted-hardening roadmap is complete. The pairing approval uses the Bee CLI registration. A relay-specific Bee registration and the remaining public-service hardening gates stay open. See the developer roadmap.
